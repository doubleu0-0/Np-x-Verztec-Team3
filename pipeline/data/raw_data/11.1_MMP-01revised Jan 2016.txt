Title: Monitoring & Measurement of Product
1. Purpose

To define the procedure for the inspection and testing of materials/products to specified product and legal requirements and the necessary controls to achieve and maintain the required standard.
To define a system for the identification of inspection and test status to ensure that only materials/products that have passed the required inspection and test are released/used.
To define a control and review system to prevent the inadvertent use or release of nonconforming materials/products and to reach a consensus on customer requirements for products and services.
2. Scope

This procedure applies to the Project PIC (Person-in-Charge) (i.e., CEO, Director of Global Operations, Manager, and Consultant).
3. Definitions

XTRANET: A network or system that allows controlled access to authorized external customers, suppliers, and other businesses.
WORK ORDER: An internal document used to initiate work, detailing customer name, project description, delivery date, etc.
Project PIC: Person-in-Charge for the Project.
Operations PIC: Person-in-Charge for Operations, as defined in Section 2 of QP-OPS-01.
4. Process Description

4.1 Monitoring and Measurement of Product:

4.1.1 The Project PIC performs monitoring and measurement of product characteristics at appropriate stages of the product realization process, aligning with planned arrangements and the product's Inspection & Test Plan.
4.1.2 This monitoring and measurement activity is carried out to verify that product requirements have been met.
4.1.3 Evidence of conformity with the acceptance criteria is maintained, and records indicate the person(s) authorizing the release of the product for delivery to the customer.
4.1.4 Product release and service delivery to the customer do not proceed until all planned arrangements have been satisfactorily completed, unless otherwise approved by the relevant authority and, where applicable, by the customer.
4.2 Control of Nonconforming Output:

4.2.1 Nonconforming outputs (e.g., products, processes, services) are identified and controlled to prevent their unintended use or delivery.
4.2.2 The Project PIC takes appropriate action based on the nature of the nonconformity and its effect on the conformity of products and services. This applies to nonconforming output at any stage of product realization.
4.2.3 Documented information is maintained for:
The nonconformity.
The actions taken.
Any concessions obtained.
The authority determining action concerning the nonconformity.
4.2.4 When a nonconforming output is corrected, it is subject to re-verification to confirm conformity with requirements.
4.2.5 Nonconforming outputs detected after delivery or during service provision are addressed appropriately.
