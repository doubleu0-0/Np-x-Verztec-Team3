ANNEX I: PROCESS INTERACTION 

The following outlines the sequence and interaction between the processes of the Management System: 

Management Levels & Their Roles:

Top Management: Involved in Management Planning (Responsibilities & Authorities, Customer & Statutory and Regulatory Requirements, Specific job/project Plans), and overall Management Planning (Responsibilities & Authorities, Policy, Objectives, Targets). They also participate in Management Programmes. 

Middle Management: Engages in Preparedness and Communication. 
Lower Management: Also involved in Preparedness and Communication. 
Management Representative(s): Involved in Preparedness and Communication. 
Key Processes & Their Interactions:

Data Analysis & Preventive Action (Risk Assessment): This process feeds into and is related to: 
Purchasing (PUR) 
Human Resources (HR) 
Contract, Process, Product, Preservation 
Customer (Interested Parties - IP) Feedback 
Calibration (CAL)* (if applicable) 
Maintenance (MTN) 
Technical/Legal Documents 
Control & Handling:
Out-of-Control Complaint / Claim Handling 
Out-of-Control Non-Conforming Product / Situation Handling 
Monitoring & Evaluation:
Internal Audit 
Inspection Supervision 
Customer (Interested Parties - IP) Satisfaction 
Improvement Cycles:
Data Analysis & Corrective Action: This process addresses issues identified in System Documents and Records. 
Management Review: This reviews System Effectiveness, Statutory and Regulatory Compliance, and Performance Achievement. 
Data Analysis & Continual Improvement (Planned Improvement): This builds on the results of management reviews. 
Abbreviations: 

IP: Interested Parties 
HR: Human Resource 
MTN: Maintenance 
R&A: Responsibilities & Authorities 
PUR: Purchasing 
CAL: Calibration 
*: If applicable
