Title: Operations/Preservations Control / Environment for the Operation of Processes / Customer & External Provider Property
1. Purpose

To define a system for identifying and planning operations, ensuring they are carried out under controlled conditions.
To establish a system for identification and traceability of materials, products, and documents throughout product realization.
To manage and control customer property.
To determine and manage the work environment needed to achieve conformity to product requirements.
2. Scope

This procedure applies to the Operations PIC (Person-in-Charge), which includes the CEO, Director of Global Operations, Manager, and Consultant.
3. Definitions

XTRANET: A network or system that allows controlled access to authorized external customers, suppliers, and other businesses.
WORK ORDER: An internal document used to initiate work, detailing customer name, project description, delivery date, etc.
Project PIC: Person-in-Charge for the Project.
Operations PIC: Person-in-Charge for Operations, as defined in Section 2.
4. Process Description

4.1 Operations Planning & Control:

4.1.1 The Sales PIC receives inquiries, prepares quotations, and obtains customer purchase orders.
4.1.2 All orders, including verbal ones, must be confirmed by a Project PIC.
4.1.3 The Project PIC confirms order acceptance by creating a work order and informing the Operations PIC to kick off the project.
4.1.4 The Operations PIC reviews the work order and assigns the job to an internal/external Project PIC (or Translator, if applicable), confirming the delivery date.
4.1.5 The Project PIC updates the project schedule on XTRANET, outlining tasks, responsibilities, and target completion dates.
4.1.6 The Project PIC ensures the project is completed by the target date, meeting requirements through effective monitoring.
4.1.7 For each project, the Project PIC prepares a Project Task Request form to confirm requirements and monitor progress.
4.1.8 The Project PIC ensures all operational processes are controlled to deliver products and services that meet requirements.
4.1.9 The Project PIC identifies and uses appropriate resources and equipment to ensure service delivery.
4.1.10 The Project PIC establishes and maintains documented information from project design and development, ensuring it covers traceability, verification, validation, and review activities.
4.2 Review of Requirements for Products & Services:

4.2.1 Before committing to provide products/services, the Operations PIC ensures:
Requirements are defined.
Contract/order requirements are different from those previously expressed.
The organization can meet the requirements.
4.2.2 Where customer requirements are not formally documented, the Operations PIC confirms them before acceptance.
4.2.3 If product/service requirements change, the Operations PIC ensures relevant documented information is amended and personnel are aware of the changes.
4.3 Property belonging to customers or external providers:

4.3.1 All customer-provided property (e.g., equipment, documents, intellectual property) for use or incorporation into products/services must be identified, verified, protected, and safeguarded.
4.3.2 Any loss, damage, or unsuitability of customer or external provider property is reported to them and documented.
4.4 Preservation of Conformity:

4.4.1 The Operations PIC preserves the conformity of products and services during processing and delivery to customer requirements.
4.4.2 This includes identification, handling, packaging, storage, transmission/transport, and protection.
4.4.3 Post-delivery activities (e.g., warranty provisions, contractual obligations, recycling) are performed when required.
4.4.4 Control of changes for production and service provision is documented, with review results, actions, and authorizations recorded.
4.4.5 The Operations PIC performs monitoring and measurement at planned stages to verify product and service requirements are met, retaining evidence of conformity and authorization for release.
4.4.6 Nonconforming outputs are identified and controlled to prevent unintended use or delivery, with actions taken based on the nature of the nonconformity.
4.4.7 The Operations PIC considers self-improvement after closing out nonconformities from internal / external audits, and proposes improvements for top management approval.
