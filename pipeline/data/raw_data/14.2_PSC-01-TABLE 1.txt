TITLE: EXTERNAL PROVIDER CONTROL PLAN 

Category A: Direct Materials (Software) 

Purchasing Method: Credit Terms / Signed Confirmation on vendor's/External Provider quotation. 
Pre-Qualification (For New External Provider): ISO certified company preferred or based on Price, Samples, Service Level, Quality/Spec. 
Responsibility for Pre-Qualification: Initiated by Purchaser, Approved by CEO/Operations PIC. 
On-Going Appraisal (Existing External Provider): Yearly Assessment. 
Responsibility for On-Going Appraisal: Purchaser. 
Quality Control For Each Order: Receiving inspection of materials by Operations PIC in accordance with contract specifications or requirements specified in the Inspection & Test Plan (ITP). Reference: ITP/External Providers D.O. 
Category B: Indirect Materials / Consumables 

Purchasing Method: Credit Terms / Signed Confirmation on vendors/External Provider quotation. 
Pre-Qualification (For New External Provider): ISO certified company preferred or based on Trial Order, Recommendation. 
Responsibility for Pre-Qualification: Initiated by Purchaser, Approved by CEO/Operations PIC. 
On-Going Appraisal (Existing External Provider): N.A. 
Responsibility for On-Going Appraisal: N.A. 
Quality Control For Each Order: Receiving verification of materials/consumables by Operations PIC in accordance with contract specifications or requirements specified in the ITP. Reference: ITP/External Providers D.O. 
Category C: Capital Purchase 

Purchasing Method: Credit Terms / Signed Confirmation on vendors/External Provider quotation. 
Pre-Qualification (For New External Provider): ISO certified company preferred or based on Showroom - Product Demo, Catalogue - Technical Evaluation. 
Responsibility for Pre-Qualification: Initiated & approved by CEO/Operations PIC. 
On-Going Appraisal (Existing External Provider): N.A. 
Responsibility for On-Going Appraisal: N.A. 
Quality Control For Each Order: Commissioning of machinery by CEO or delegate to ensure conformance to contract specifications. 
Category D: Subcontract Works (Outsourced) (Translators, Interpreters, Voice-Overs, Translation Agencies, Copywriters) 

Purchasing Method: P.O. 
Pre-Qualification (For New External Provider): Passed Sample test reviewed internally/by senior translators, ISO certified company preferred or based on Recommendation, Market Reputation, Portfolio of past experiences & clients. 
Responsibility for Pre-Qualification: Initiated by Purchaser, Approved by CEO/Operations PIC. 
On-Going Appraisal (Existing External Provider): Yearly Assessment. 
Responsibility for On-Going Appraisal: Purchaser/Operations PIC. 
Quality Control For Each Order: Ongoing deliverables check by Purchaser/Operations PIC in accordance with contract specifications or requirements specified in the Operations Control Plan. Inspection records shall be maintained. 
Category E: Services (Outsourced) (Maintenance of Office Equipment) 

Purchasing Method: Credit Terms / Signed Confirmation on vendors/External Provider quotation/contract. 
Pre-Qualification (For New External Provider): ISO certified company preferred or based on Track Record, Recommendation. 
Responsibility for Pre-Qualification: Initiated by Purchaser, Approved by CEO/Operations PIC. 
On-Going Appraisal (Existing External Provider): Yearly Assessment. 
Responsibility for On-Going Appraisal: Purchaser/Operations PIC. 
Quality Control For Each Order: Verification of services rendered/Servicing Report/D.O. by Purchaser/Operations PIC upon handover by service contractor.
