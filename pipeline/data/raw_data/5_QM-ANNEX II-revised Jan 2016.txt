FUNCTION: MANAGEMENT RESPONSIBILITY

TOP MANAGEMENT

ISO 9001 QUALITY RESPONSIBILITY:

-To provide commitment evidence to QMS and effectiveness improvement:
Organization Communication: importance of meeting customer & statutory and regulatory requirements
Establishment - Quality Policy
Ensure establishment - measurable Quality Objectives at relevant functions & levels
Conduct Management Review
Ensure Resource Availability
-To ensure customer requirements are determined & met for enhancing customer satisfaction.
-To ensure QMS Planning and Integrity - despite changes.
-To ensure Responsibility & Authority defined & communicated within organization.
-To appoint Management Representative.
-To ensure establishment of internal communication processes, including QMS & its effectiveness.
-To review QMS at planned intervals for effectiveness, continual improvement & changes.
To be accountable for the effectiveness of the quality management system.

AUTHORITY (PIC):
-CEO
-Management Representative

(Quality Manual)

PURCHASING

ISO 9001 QUALITY RESPONSIBILITY:

-To evaluate & select suppliers based on their ability to meet requirements - with selection, evaluation & re-evaluation criteria established; to maintain evaluation-result records.

requirements for approval of products, procedures, processes, equipment, personnel qualification, QMS
-To establish & implement inspection or other activities:
To ensure purchased product meets specified requirements
-To state intended verification of organization or customer and product release method in purchasing info.
-To identify & control all outsourced process for product conformity.

FUNCTION: PRODUCT REALIZATION (LINE FUNCTIONS)

PROCESS & SERVICE (OPERATIONS CONTROL)

ISO 9001 QUALITY RESPONSIBILITY:
-To plan & carry out production or service, under controlled conditions - including product characteristics, suitable equipment, MM (Monitoring & Measurement IMM), product release, delivery & post-delivery activities
-To ensure supplier control for outsourcing work
-To validate "special" processes
-To provide positive product identification & traceability, throughout product realization
-To obtain and maintain identification of a product and maintain record, where traceability is a requirement
-To preserve the product by backing up data in the server for storage, and protect from virus using anti-virus software
-To identify, verify, protect and safeguard customer property, including Intellectual Property, to report to customer if customer property is lost, damaged or unsuitable for use and maintain record
-To train, brief & supervise work and to control process to Process Plans
-To implement product release, delivery & post-delivery activities
-To supervise & manage work environment for product requirements conformity
-To plan & implement Work Supervision or Process Control (Process Monitoring & Measurement) • Vendor Control • Customer Satisfaction • Data Analysis • Improvement process for product & QMS conformity and QMS effectiveness improvement - methods shall include statistical technique applicability
-To determine, collect & analyze data for informed management decision
-To commend for effective actions to prevent recurrence (corrective action) and prevent occurrence (preventive action) while continually improve QMS effectiveness

-Manager

-Design Team
