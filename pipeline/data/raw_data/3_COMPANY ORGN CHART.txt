VERZTEC CONSULTING PTE LTD COMPANY ORGANIZATION CHART

The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) holds the highest authority at Verztec Consulting Pte Ltd and oversees all departments, operations, and strategic initiatives.

Reporting directly to the CEO is the Chief Operating Officer (COO), who manages daily business operations. The COO directly oversees the Admin & Operations and Finance departments.

Within Admin & Operations, the Senior Operations Manager reports to the COO and supervises the Admin & Receptionist. The Human Resource department is also managed under the Senior Operations Manager.

The Finance department, under the COO’s supervision, includes the Accounts team, which handles budgeting, financial reporting, and company transactions.

The Service Delivery division reports directly to the CEO. It includes the QA/QC team, which ensures service quality, compliance, and process improvement.

The Project Management structure operates within the Service Delivery function. It consists of Directors and Managers who lead project execution, Senior Consultants and Consultants who offer expert insights, and Translators and Script Writers who manage content development and localisation.

The Procurement department also reports to the CEO. It includes an Admin Officer who oversees Resource Coordinators, responsible for managing material sourcing and resource allocation.

The IT department is directly under the CEO and led by the IT Team Leader, who supervises a Designer, Web Developer, Applications Engineer, and Multimedia Developer to maintain technical infrastructure and create digital content.

The Marketing department, reporting to the CEO, comprises a Marketing Manager and Marketing Executive responsible for brand strategy, promotional campaigns, and public outreach.

The Business Development team also reports to the CEO. It includes a Business Development Manager and Business Development Executive, both focused on client acquisition and new market opportunities.

While each department has its designated leads, the CEO ultimately oversees all operations, personnel, and service functions across the organisation.
