Management Foreword / Commitment 


This is the The Quality Manual and its procedures are based on ISO 9001:2015. The quality manual is the representative of all ISO files.
The main purpose of the ISO files are for the company to:
a) demonstrate our ability to provide consistently product that meets customer, statutory and regulatory requirements applicable to the product & service, and
b) aims to enhance customer satisfaction through the effective application of the system, including processes for continual improvement of the system and the assurance of conformity to customer, statutory and regulatory requirements applicable to the product & service.
The Steering Committee, appointed by Top Management and responsible for developing this manual, consists of the following representatives:
1) Mr Nicholas Goh – CEO
2) Ms Jenny Woon – COO


Top Management
The top management is committed to the development and improvement of the quality management system by :
- Ensuring customer, statutory, and regulatory requirements are identified and met to enhance customer satisfaction.
- Communicating the importance of meeting these requirements throughout the organization.
- Establishing the quality policy and quality objectives.
- Ensuring planning and integrity of the Quality Management System (QMS).
- Defining and communicating responsibilities and authorities.
- Appointing management representative(s).
- Conducting management reviews.
- Integrating QMS requirements into business processes.
- Promoting the use of the process approach and risk-based thinking.
- Ensuring resources for the QMS are available.
- Communicating the importance of effective quality management and conformity to QMS requirements.
- Ensuring the QMS achieves its intended results.
- Engaging, directing, and supporting personnel to contribute to QMS effectiveness.
- Promoting continual improvement.
- Supporting other management roles to demonstrate leadership.
- Taking accountability for QMS effectiveness.
- Ensuring the quality policy and objectives are compatible with the organization's context.


Customer Focus 
Top management will demonstrate leadership and commitment with respect to customer focus by ensuring that:
a) Customer and applicable statutory and regulatory requirements are determined, understood and consistently met
b) The risks and opportunities that can affect conformity of products and services and the ability to enhance customer satisfaction are determined and addressed
c) The focus on enhancing customer satisfaction is maintained


Appointment of Management Representative (MR) 
The CEO appoints an overall-in-charge, designated as the Management Representative (MR), to ensure QMS requirements are established, implemented, and maintained. Ms. Jenny Woon (COO) is appointed as the MR. A Deputy Management Representative (DMR) may be appointed to assist the MR. The DMR position is current vacant. The MR ensures QMS processes are established, implemented, and maintained, reports on QMS performance, promotes awareness of customer requirements, and liaises with external parties.


Quality Policy 
The Management and Staff of VERZTEC CONSULTING PTE LTD are committed to achieve Enhanced Customer Satisfaction by satisfying applicable requirements and Continual Improvement of the Quality Management System through :
1.	On-Time Delivery 
2.	Quality Service
We shall work as a team to constantly maintain and continually improve our management quality – using ISO 9001 : 2015 Quality Management System as a management tool.

INTRODUCTION  (ABOUT THE COMPANY)
Company Profile
We are a customer-focused organization. Verztec is dedicated in delivering globalization solutions that satisfy our customers' business imperatives.  We conduct customer satisfaction surveys and use the feedback to tune our business so that we are continuously improving our ability to support our customers.  We work collaboratively with our customers to innovate as this forms a significant part of our business model.
Scope of Business
Our scope of business includes :
Localization and Translation, Interpretation,  Internationalization, Software Localization & Testing, Website Localization, e-Learning and Training applications, Audio Recording,  Multilingual Desktop Publishing, Transcription, Globalization Consulting and Web Engineering, Design and development of annual reports, marketing collaterals, websites, e-Learning etc. When determining this scope, Verztec Consulting Pte Ltd  will consider:
- Internal issues such as strong technical capability, adequate equipment, low employee turnover, and the need for guidance for new staff.
- External issues including market competitiveness, cultural diversity, political stability, workforce availability, legal compliance, and climate change driving more digital solutions.
- The products and services the organization delivers.
- The needs of relevant interested parties, including:
-- Customers, who require timely delivery and services that meet quality and contractual expectations.
-- Employees, who expect fair compensation, training, clear processes, and career development.
-- Vendors and external providers, who need clear requirements, prompt payment, and open communication.
-- Regulators, who require legal compliance (e.g., WSH and data protection laws).
-- Certification bodies, who assess conformance to ISO 9001:2015.
-- Competitors, who drive the need to stay competitive and meet evolving customer needs.
