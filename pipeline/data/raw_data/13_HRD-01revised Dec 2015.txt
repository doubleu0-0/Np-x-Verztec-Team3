HRD-01 Revised December 2015 Document Summary
This document outlines the procedure for ensuring a competent workforce and addressing training needs within the organization.

1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to:

Define the process for identifying competent levels and training needs.
Ensure suitable training programs are designed and provided for all personnel whose activities affect product requirements.
Evaluate the effectiveness of the training provided.
2. Scope
This procedure applies to personnel overseeing staff training needs, particularly for those whose work impacts product requirements. It aims to ensure that staff possess the necessary knowledge for operational processes and to achieve conformity of products and services.

3. Responsibility

3.1 Management Representative (or Designate)
The Management Representative is responsible for:

Identifying the competency level of all personnel affecting product requirements.
Identifying training needs based on the competency level.
Arranging necessary training programs or other actions to fulfill training needs.
Ensuring relevant training records are updated.
Evaluating the effectiveness of training provided.
3.2 All Personnel
All personnel are required to:

Participate in training programs as dictated by their job function.
Ensure their training records are kept up-to-date.
4. Procedure

4.1 Competency, Awareness & Training

4.1.1 The Management Representative (or designate) establishes the required competency level for all personnel affecting product requirements (refer to Annex I for details).
4.1.2 Training needs are identified based on competency levels, and a determination is made if personnel require formal courses or on-job-training (OJT).
4.1.3 Awareness is raised among personnel regarding the importance of their activities and their contribution to quality objectives.
4.1.4 Training records (including education, experience, skills, and training) for all personnel are maintained (refer to Annex II).
4.1.5 Training effectiveness is evaluated periodically through audits (internal/external), examinations, or practical tests.
4.2 Review and Continual Improvement
The Management Review Meeting evaluates the following:

Effectiveness of training provided.
Competency of the personnel.
Adequacy and effectiveness of staff training requirements.
Training budget.
